# TFast Audience Strategy

Design the audience plan. Sources: Client Brain `customers` (personas, demographics, objections), `advertising_history` (what worked before), platform guardrail skills for mechanics.

## Principles

1. **Start broad** — TikTok and Meta reward broad targeting; the algorithm finds the buyer better than hand-stacked interests. Avoid stacking more than 2–3 interest signals.
2. **Lookalikes scale** — 1% lookalike of purchasers is the highest-value cold audience. Expand outward (1% → 3% → 5%) only after the tighter pool is profitable.
3. **Custom audiences do two jobs** — retargeting (site visitors, engagers) and exclusion (existing customers out of prospecting).
4. **Personas → language, not targeting** — personas guide creative and copy; the platform handles the distribution.

## Per-platform notes

| Platform | Guidance |
|---|---|
| TikTok | Broad or minimal interest. Creative is the targeting. |
| Meta | Broad + Advantage+; lookalikes for scale; detailed targeting as exception |
| Google Ads | Keywords/audience signals — intent is the targeting |
